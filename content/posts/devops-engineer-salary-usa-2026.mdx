## Why DevOps pays a premium

Computer and IT occupations sit well above the national median — about $105,990 per the [BLS](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/computer-and-information-technology/) — and DevOps trends toward the upper end. The work spans software and operations, so the talent pool is thinner, and reliability and deployment velocity map directly to revenue. That combination of scarcity and business impact is what sets the price, the same dynamic visible in [big-tech leveling](/blog/faang-software-engineer-compensation-2026).

## What the market pays for

Specific, demonstrable depth: one cloud mastered to architecture level, infrastructure-as-code (Terraform), CI/CD pipelines, containers and Kubernetes, and observability. [Skillsoft](https://www.skillsoft.com/blog/top-paying-aws-certifications) data puts the AWS DevOps Engineer Professional around $164,012 globally — among the highest-paying credentials, and a strong signal in the US market.

## DevOps vs platform engineering

The titles overlap but are diverging — platform engineering increasingly means building internal developer platforms, while DevOps spans the broader culture and pipeline. The distinction (and its pay implications) is worth understanding; see [platform engineer vs DevOps](/blog/platform-engineer-vs-devops-2026).

## The roadmap

Pick one cloud and go deep — the [cloud certifications ROI guide](/blog/cloud-certifications-worth-it-2026) shows the sequence. Layer IaC, a CI/CD pipeline, and Kubernetes, then deploy a real application end to end with monitoring and automated rollback. Pairing DevOps with AI infrastructure (MLOps) — adjacent to the [US AI engineer roadmap](/blog/ai-engineer-roadmap-usa-2026) — opens an even higher band.

## The directive

Master one cloud, automate one real deployment end to end with observability, and document your reliability and cost decisions. That depth is what moves a DevOps engineer to the top of one of the most durable, well-paid tracks in US tech.
