For a developer who wants to work in the United States, treating the H-1B lottery as the only door is a strategic error. There are several routes, and the smart move is to build a profile that opens more than one.

This article is general career and market information, not legal or immigration advice. Visa eligibility is fact-specific — always confirm your situation with a qualified immigration attorney and the official sources below.

## The H-1B reality

The H-1B is the best-known route for specialty occupations, but [USCIS](https://www.uscis.gov/working-in-the-united-states/h-1b-specialty-occupations) administers it under an annual numerical cap, and in high-demand years selection runs by lottery. That means a strong candidate can simply not be selected. It remains worth pursuing — but only as one branch of a plan, not the whole tree.

## The alternatives worth knowing

**O-1 (extraordinary ability)** has no annual lottery; it rewards a documented record — publications, open-source impact, talks, recognition. For developers building a public body of work, this is a route worth cultivating deliberately. See the [USCIS O-1 overview](https://www.uscis.gov/working-in-the-united-states/temporary-workers/o-1-visa-individuals-with-extraordinary-ability-or-achievement).

**L-1 (intracompany transfer)** lets a multinational move an existing employee to a U.S. office. This is exactly why working at a multinational — including the parent of an [Indian GCC](/blog/india-gcc-jobs-2026) — can become an immigration path over time.

**Cap-exempt H-1B** employers, such as certain universities and affiliated nonprofits, are not subject to the lottery, offering a steadier route for the right roles.

## Build a profile that qualifies for more than one

The throughline across these routes is the same thing that lifts pay: a scarce, demonstrable record of capability. The skills our [FAANG compensation breakdown](/blog/faang-software-engineer-compensation-2026) flags as accelerators — and the public, verifiable work that supports an O-1 — also make you a stronger H-1B and L-1 candidate. If U.S. routes stay uncertain, the [EU Blue Card](/blog/eu-blue-card-tech-relocation-2026) is a parallel option, and [USD remote work](/blog/remote-developer-jobs-india-usd-2026) lets you capture global pay without relocating at all.
