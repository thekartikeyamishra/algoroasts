## What the Junior Gap actually is

You cannot shortcut the years of context that turn a junior programmer into an architect — but AI can shortcut the *tasks* juniors used to be hired for. When those tasks vanish, so does the on-ramp. The result is an industry that under-hires juniors today and discovers a senior shortage in a decade.
