## Route 1: remote employment abroad

Foreign companies — including the parent organizations behind many [Indian GCCs](/blog/india-gcc-jobs-2026) — hire fully remote engineers and pay closer to home-market bands. The bar is high and the competition is global, so this route rewards the same scarce depth that lifts [domestic GCC pay](/blog/india-software-developer-salary-2026): one platform mastered, one AI competency, and a system you have owned end to end.

## Route 2: freelance and contract platforms

Global freelance and contract marketplaces let you bill in USD per hour or per project. The mistake most Indian freelancers make is anchoring rates to local expectations. Price against global benchmarks instead — the [US contractor rate guide](/blog/software-contractor-rates-usa-2026) and the [EU day-rate guide](/blog/eu-contractor-day-rates-2026) give you the reference points to negotiate from.

## Route 3: direct client retainers

The highest-margin route is owning the client relationship directly — monthly retainers for a specialized service. It demands sales and delivery discipline, but it removes the platform's cut and lets you compound reputation. A [cloud certification with real depth](/blog/cloud-certification-roi-india-2026) is a credible signal that shortens the trust gap with a new overseas client.

## Pricing and the money plumbing

Two non-negotiables. First, **price in USD against global rates** — your cost of living is your advantage, not a reason to discount. Second, **get the plumbing right**: receive foreign income through proper banking channels and report it under Indian law. The [Reserve Bank of India](https://www.rbi.org.in/) publishes the framework governing foreign inward remittance; this is not legal advice, so confirm your specific situation with a qualified chartered accountant.
