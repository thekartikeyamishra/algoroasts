## The German tech market

Germany combines a large enterprise base (automotive, industrial, finance), a deep startup scene in Berlin, and a steady stream of international employers. Salaries are strong and stable rather than explosive — closer to European norms than to [US big-tech total comp](/blog/faang-software-engineer-compensation-2026), but paired with robust benefits, strong labor protections, and (outside Munich) reasonable living costs.

## Cities and character

Berlin is the startup and scale-up hub with an international, English-friendly scene. Munich skews enterprise and deep-tech with higher pay and higher costs. Hamburg and Frankfurt round out the market with media, logistics, and finance. The [Berlin vs Amsterdam comparison](/blog/berlin-vs-amsterdam-tech-2026) is worth reading if you're choosing between EU hubs.

## Relocating as a non-EU developer

The [EU Blue Card](/blog/eu-blue-card-tech-relocation-2026) is the primary route, and Germany's reforms made it notably accessible: per the [European Commission](https://home-affairs.ec.europa.eu/policies/migration-and-asylum/legal-migration-and-resettlement/work/eu-blue-card-attracting-highly-qualified-talent-eu_en) framework, 2025 thresholds sit around €48,300 for general occupations and about €43,759.80 for shortage occupations including IT, with experience-based eligibility for IT professionals without a degree. Always verify the current figure before applying.

## Contracting as an alternative

If permanent employment isn't your goal, contracting is well-established in Germany, especially for enterprise clients — see the [EU contractor day-rate guide](/blog/eu-contractor-day-rates-2026) for how to price. Rates for cloud, AI, and security specialists run well above permanent equivalents.

## The directive

Target Berlin, Munich, or Hamburg based on whether you want startup energy or enterprise stability, benchmark your salary against the local market rather than your home country, and use the Blue Card's accessible thresholds if relocating from outside the EU. Verify all figures with official sources.
